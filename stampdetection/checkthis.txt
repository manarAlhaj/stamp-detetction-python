install ultralytics first, these were my configs for predicting on the test images., save_crop isnt needed i just used it to crop the stamps for the data synth im doing.

from ultralytics import YOLO
bestone = YOLO('/content/drive/MyDrive/StampDetection/runs/v6/weights/best.pt')

preds = bestone.predict(
    source='/content/drive/MyDrive/StampDetection/allstamps',
    conf=0.5,
    save=True,
    iou=0.45,
    imgsz=960,
    project='/content/drive/MyDrive/StampDetection/runs',
    name='croppedstamps',
    save_crop =True
    
)
